## Waves 2–5 (one screen)
- **Niche:** no immersive world game has Arabic-for-Quran at its core. LingoLooper and Mondly VR include Arabic generically. Market rewards depth: one language, one neighbourhood (Wagotabi model).
- **Adults learn from screens:** video teacher ≥ live teacher (Martin 2019, small n). Hard rule removes faces → body language + Arabic captions carry meaning.
- **Form focus helps:** g = 1.06 over 54 studies; explicit vs implicit gap small. Prompts beat recasts (Lyster & Saito 2010).
- **Spacing:** ~10 spaced encounters; FSRS; test after sleep. Delayed confirmation frustrates (So to Speak) → confirm guesses quickly.
- **Captions:** Arabic captions ≈ 4× gain of English subtitles. Full tashkeel from day one.
- **Honesty:** fluency claims unsupported; ~9,000 lemmas for 95% MSA coverage.
- **Quran curve:** ~75 lemmas → 50%, ~300 → 70%, ~1,000 → 85%, ~1,600 → 90% (reconstructed; must recount from Tanzil). Fekry's 85% = ~283 words + verb patterns.
- **Register:** Fusha core; "educated white Arabic" spoken; Egyptian/Levantine listen-only.
- **Pronunciation:** 96% of learner errors are substitutions (IU Madinah); teach by makhārij + minimal pairs.
- **Licensing:** Tanzil verbatim + credit; fonts via Quran Foundation; QuranEnc translations; no QAC data; no recitation audio without a commissioned reciter.
- **Etiquette:** never cut verses; sanctuary contexts only; mute for adhan (Mirage lesson).
- **Speech:** Job 1 (pick 1 of 2–4 lines) is feasible; iPhone Whisper unproven; Job 2 needs phoneme models (IqraEval F1 0.72).
- **Stack:** R3F + Spark splats, WebGL2 baseline; strict budgets; Cloudflare Pages/R2; Tauri; Capacitor later ($99 flagged).
- **Story:** composite modern student; Ibn Battuta legends from public-domain Arabic only.
